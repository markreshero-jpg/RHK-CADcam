## Lessons (for the draw-plan skill)
- Locked inner-drawer `y_position` is in the cabinet frame — it includes the kick until the kicks are JOINED (then the frame starts at
  the carcass bottom). Join kicks before setting inner drawers / hinges, or add the kick height.
- Benchtop `height_mm` = top of the carcass (880 here), not the finished bench height.
- Face grids are uniform rows x cols (no spanning zones) — a full-width panel across a 2-col face = custom Applied Panel + 'open' zones.
- A WOT kit's D2-LL can be swapped to one drawer + its own kick joined into the run by setting kick_run_id / has_toekick / pos_z by hand.
